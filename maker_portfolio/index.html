I am an architect with the soul of a craftsman.

me

me

I am a maker at heart. My journey through architecture and digital fabrication has always been driven by one simple question: "How can it be made?"
My years building furniture taught me to work with millimeter precision, to be meticulous, to understand that the beauty of an object lies in the truth of its assembly.
That is the approach I bring to architectural models. For me, it's not just about building a representation; it's about understanding the design through my hands. From the first line in a CAD model to the final cut on the CNC or the last touch with a hand tool, that's where I find my purpose.
